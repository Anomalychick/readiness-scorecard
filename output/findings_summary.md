# Findings: AI Assistant HAL rollout at Monolith National Bank

**SYNTHETIC DATA.** All people, numbers and the bank are made up. Data runs 2026-01-05 to 2026-05-24; go-live 2026-03-02.

## Summary

- 1 of 6 departments is on track, 2 to watch, and 3 need action.
- Training is where behavior changed: across the bank, employees used the tool on 14.1% of workdays before their own training and 32.1% after.
- Where the chain from training to results breaks: Branch Operations at reach, adoption; Lending at readiness, adoption, outcomes; Deposit Operations at outcomes.

### Branch Operations: Needs action

Reach ✕ Red Readiness ! Amber Adoption ✕ Red Outcomes ! Amber

**What's working**

- Training paid off: use rose from 12.1% to 25.0% of workdays after people were trained.

**What isn't**

- Training ran late: 77.6% finished on time (26 of 116 eligible employees finished after the deadline). 50.9% registered only after the reminder email.
- Only 33.3% answered the latest survey, so its 77.6% favorable score may not represent the department.
- 6 employees used the tool on zero days in the last 30, so only 94.8% met the minimum (needs 97%). That turns adoption red even though 53.0% are heavy users.

**Watch**

- Transaction processing time improved 2.5% vs. baseline: amber (green is 7.5%).

**Recommended next actions**

- Protected training time: schedule coverage so staff can step away to train, and block the time on calendars.
- Manager accountability: send managers a weekly completion list before the deadline, not only the escalation after it.
- Manager follow-up: managers encourage survey completion, without seeing individual answers.
- Manager accountability: give managers the list of employees with no prompts in the last 30 days.

## How this summary is written

Each sentence comes from a fixed rule applied to the scorecard data (for example: if on-time training is below green, report it and recommend the late-training actions). Recommended actions come from the playbook in the settings file. The rules never see how the synthetic data was generated; they read only the calculated metrics.
