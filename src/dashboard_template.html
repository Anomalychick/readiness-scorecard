About this project

After every rollout, leaders ask the same question: did this change actually take hold, and where do we need to act? This scorecard answers it with four measures, scored by department, and shows where the chain from training to results breaks.

What it measures

  • Reach: did people get the emails and complete mandatory training on time?
  • Readiness: do they feel prepared and positive? (optional 7-point surveys)
  • Adoption: are they really using the tool, or just checking the box?
  • Outcomes: did the department's business result improve?

Rules drawn from real bank practice

  • Mandatory training escalates misses to skip-level managers, so green means 97%+ on time.
  • Anyone on leave during a measurement window is excluded from the rate and shown as a count.
  • Tool use appears on performance scorecards, so one prompt a month "meets"; 5+ active days "exceeds".
  • If fewer than 97% meet the minimum, adoption is red regardless.

How it was built

Built with Claude Code: requirements, review, testing and every business rule directed by the author.

Synthetic data is generated with a fixed seed. Each department follows a planted pattern; the SQL has to find it from the data alone, and the tests confirm it does.
